The honest bit

A photo taken by a human camera contains no actual ultraviolet. We cannot invent data nobody recorded, and any startup that tells you otherwise is lying to you at a valuation.

What we do instead: model the reflectance curve running through the three wavelengths your camera did sample, then read it off where a Korveth eye would look. It is a re-encoding, not a photograph. Happily, that is exactly what the job needs — the problem was never that your memo lacks ultraviolet detail, it is that your monitor emits no ultraviolet whatsoever. To a Korveth your quarterly review is a slab of warm grey nothing. We move the information somewhere they can reach it.

Series A roadmap

The transform in assets/uv-transform.js is pure, dependency-free and has never seen a DOM in its life, so the same file can run on a server unchanged. Next up: POST /v1/translate for stills and a WebSocket channel for live content, so we can stream to a whole floor of Korveths at once. Since the whole transform is one 3×3 matrix, we ship the matrix and let the client do the work — extremely cheap, which we will be describing as “edge-native” in the deck.

Nothing you load here leaves your machine. Every pixel is processed in your browser. We are not currently monetising your holiday photos, which the investors consider a missed opportunity.
